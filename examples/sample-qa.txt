To deploy checkout-api, run `kubectl apply -f k8s/checkout-api.yaml -n prod`
from the release branch, then watch the rollout with
`kubectl rollout status deployment/checkout-api -n prod`. A deployment is

happen after the deploy. If payments is down, checkout-api returns an error instead of
taking a payment it cannot complete. It runs as a Kubernetes deployment in the prod namespace
with three replicas.
