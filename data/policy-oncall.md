# On-Call Policy

> Fictional sample document. Acme Retail is a made-up company; nothing here
> describes a real client, system, or incident.

## Rotations

The on-call rotation lasts one week, from Monday 09:00 to the following
Monday 09:00. The primary on-call engineer carries the pager for that week.
A secondary on-call engineer is on standby for the same week and covers
holidays and sick leave.

## Response times

A page must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. If a page is not acknowledged
in 15 minutes, it escalates automatically to the secondary on-call engineer.
A critical incident must have a human working on it within 30 minutes of the
first page.

## Escalation

If the secondary on-call engineer does not respond within 15 minutes, the
page escalates to the engineering manager. The engineering manager may call
in extra engineers or declare a major incident. No incident may sit
unacknowledged for more than one hour.
